How quickly will I hear back?
Most inquiries receive a reply within 2 hours during office hours. Overnight messages are answered the following morning by 9 AM GMT.
Can I book immediately?
Yes — for direct bookings, head to our Stays collection. Submit the form here for bespoke requests, special rates, or stays that aren't publicly listed.

Absolutely. We host business travelers and long-stay guests regularly, and offer preferential rates for extended stays. Select Partnership as your reason, and a specialist will follow up.
Is my information private?
Yes. All inquiries are confidential and stored securely. We never share your details with third parties, and you can request deletion at any time.
