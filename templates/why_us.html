Good to Know

Frequently asked

How do I book an estate?
Browse our Stays collection, pick your estate, and click "Book". You'll get a confirmation from our concierge within 2 hours. You can also submit a bespoke inquiry for stays not listed publicly.
What's included in the nightly rate?
Every stay includes the full private residence, high-speed Wi-Fi, premium linens, and 24/7 concierge support. Optional extras (private chef, airport transfer, driver) can be arranged on request.
Is the location really 15 minutes from the airport?
Yes — most of our estates are a 15-minute drive from Kotoka International Airport, depending on traffic. Airport pickup can be arranged for an additional fee.
Can I book for a business trip or longer stay?
Absolutely. We host business travelers and long-stay guests regularly. Extended stays (7+ nights) qualify for preferential rates — contact our concierge for a custom quote.
What if I need to cancel?
Cancel free up to 7 days before check-in. Cancellations within 7 days are subject to a 50% fee. Full policy is shared with your booking confirmation.
