Meet the Founder

Hi — I'm Kwame Boakye

Mr. Dave Africa — founder of Le Rêve Properties and a Pan-African entrepreneur based in Accra, Ghana. I started Le Rêve with a simple belief: that everyone who comes to Africa deserves a place that feels like home — comfortable, welcoming, and genuinely affordable. Not a transaction. A real experience.

I've personally hosted over 100 guests from across Ghana and around the world — business travelers, families, diaspora returning home, explorers arriving for the first time. Every single one of them has taught me something about what great hospitality actually means. That's why Le Rêve exists: not just as a place to stay, but as a space where people feel seen, valued, and genuinely taken care of.

Beyond hospitality, I work as a Pan-African trade connector — helping African businesses expand across borders, build trusted partnerships, and access new markets. That wider mission shapes everything about Le Rêve: we're not just renting rooms, we're building the hospitality infrastructure Africa deserves — one home, one partnership, one guest at a time.

"We're not building a rental business. We're building a reason to come home to Africa."

— Kwame Boakye, Founder

From Airbnb to our own platform

Our hosting started on Airbnb. Now we're building Le Rêve — a platform that takes everything we learned hosting 100+ guests and turns it into an open network for all of Ghana.
